h1 to h6

The <h1> to <h6> tags define headings in HTML.

  • ✔ <h1> is the highest (biggest) level heading.
  • ✔ <h6> is the smallest heading.

Headings help in SEO, page structure, and accessibility.

Example Code:

<h1>Heading 1</h1>
<h2>Heading 2</h2>
<h3>Heading 3</h3>
<h4>Heading 4</h4>
<h5>Heading 5</h5>
<h6>Heading 6</h6>
